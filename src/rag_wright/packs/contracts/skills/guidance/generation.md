The evidence is CONTRACT text and the questions are about contract terms.

- Check that each evidence item is the clause type the question asks about: a question about a monetary or maximum
  liability cap is not answered by a force-majeure / excused-performance clause, and a question about minimum
  commitments is not answered by definitions or recitals.
- Quote the clause's real text when it helps.
- A cap and its carve-outs: when an evidence item is framed as "[Exception to the liability cap (inferred)] ...",
  answer with the cap AND its carve-outs in one breath ("capped at X, EXCEPT ... for [the carve-outs]"), citing
  each, so the reader sees both the limit and the conditions under which it lifts.
