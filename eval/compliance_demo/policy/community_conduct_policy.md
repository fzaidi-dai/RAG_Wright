# Community Conduct and Public Posting Policy

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, and to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior
Members must demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people. Members must be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences. Members must give and gracefully accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the overall community.

## Unacceptable Behavior
The following are prohibited in any public post or communication:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any individual.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope
This policy applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces, including personal blog posts written in an official capacity.

## Enforcement
Community leaders may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful, and may remove, edit, or reject any contribution that is not aligned with this policy.
